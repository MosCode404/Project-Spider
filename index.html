BIRD SPIDERS

What are bird spiders?

Bird spider is a common term associated with tarantulas. Tarantulas are large spiders belonging primarily to the family Theraphosidae and are found in many parts of the world.

BASIC CARE

Responsible tarantula keeping

Every species has different requirements. Before keeping a tarantula, research its enclosure, ventilation, substrate, temperature, humidity, feeding and hydration needs.

Species-specific information should always take priority over general care advice.

Tarantulas should be observed rather than unnecessarily handled, and their enclosure should provide a secure, suitable environment.
