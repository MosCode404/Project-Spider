WHAT ARE BIRD SPIDERS?

Tarantulas are extraordinary arachnids.

Bird spiders are commonly known as tarantulas. They belong to the family Theraphosidae and can be found across many parts of the world.

BASIC CARE

Understanding your bird spider

Responsible tarantula keeping starts with understanding the species you keep.


Housing, ventilation, substrate, temperature, humidity, feeding and access to clean water should all be considered according to the requirements of the individual species.


Always research a species thoroughly before acquiring one.
