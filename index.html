ABOUT BIRD SPIDERS

More than a spider. A fascinating world.

Bird spiders, commonly associated with tarantulas, are among the most fascinating arachnids on Earth.

From colorful arboreal species to powerful terrestrial burrowers, their diversity is extraordinary.

Bird Spider World combines species information with responsible keeping and a carefully presented selection of available animals.

BIRD SPIDER KNOWLEDGE

Understand your spider

01

Habitat

Bird spiders occupy diverse warm environments. Some are terrestrial, while others spend much of their lives above ground.

02

Behavior

Individual behavior varies considerably. Retreating, burrowing, web-building and defensive displays can all occur.

03

Molting

Bird spiders grow by shedding their exoskeleton. The period around a molt is particularly vulnerable.

04

Anatomy

Eight legs, specialized mouthparts, sensory hairs and powerful adaptations make these animals remarkable predators.

RESPONSIBLE KEEPING

Before you bring one home.

01

Research the species

Different bird spiders have different environmental and behavioral requirements.

02

Prepare the enclosure

Housing, ventilation, substrate, security and environmental conditions should be prepared beforehand.

03

Understand feeding

Bird spiders are predators and their feeding requirements vary by species, age and condition.

04

Respect the animal

Bird spiders are fascinating animals, but they should be observed and handled responsibly.
